## 🕹️ About

Wild West Pioneers is a single-player city-building and strategy game set on the North American frontier during the pioneering era. Lead settlers from a small wagon camp to thriving towns by managing housing, resources, production chains, trade, and expansion. Developed by EmpireCraft Studios and published by Toplitz Productions, the game is planned for Early Access on September 25, 2026.

Wild West Pioneers is for players who enjoy relaxed but detailed city builders, colony management, resource logistics, and frontier strategy.

## ✨ Features

- 🏗️ **Frontier City Building** — Grow a settlement from a basic camp into a prosperous pioneer town.
- 🌾 **Resource Management** — Provide food, water, housing, health, and other essentials for your settlers.
- ⚙️ **Production Chains** — Establish connected production systems for materials, supplies, and town development.
- 🧑‍🌾 **Living Settlers** — Manage individual pioneers with personal needs, jobs, and happiness.
- 🗺️ **Strategic Expansion** — Scout the frontier, establish outposts, and found additional settlements.
- 🚚 **Trade Routes** — Connect settlements and outposts through trade and transportation networks.
- 🏆 **Sandbox Strategy** — Shape each playthrough through flexible settlement planning and expansion.
